QUALITY CONTROL

Our team members have more than ten years of experience in export-import procedures. Even we have global work experience, or multi-national companies, sector leaders; we always experience some quality problems or some customer dissatisfaction.

MakeMattress is a trader company. Trader companies sometimes put you in trouble about quality control or loading method or something similar points. Because we are supplying your order from different companies and loading to you. Even in our process, we had experience tons of time in these scenarios. After all these stories we set our own quality control departments. We have our standard quality control data, more than that you can share your special request like pallet size, like packaging methods, like loading methods, etc.

Let us done this operation under all these conditions. We dont want to push our rules and conditions; trade is a mutual organization. You should know the producer company, production method, quality control conditions, loading process, logistic operations, and more...

Our mentality is transparency in operations. Ask our quality control standards for each product. And feel comfortable with every single process.
