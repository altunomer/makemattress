3. DESIGN SYSTEM & UI/UX PHILOSOPHY (LIQUID GLASS MINIMALISM)
================================================================================
Inspired by the quiet luxury, generous whitespace, and refined elegance of Malouf Home, combined with modern Apple-grade liquid glassmorphism:
- Typography:
  * Primary Font: `Roboto` (Google Fonts: 300, 400, 500, 700, 900) across body, headings, cards, and UI components.
  * Serif Accents: `Cormorant Garamond` (available for specialized editorial flourishes).
- Image & Visual Rules:
  * 100% Full Color & Vibrant: No grayscale, black & white, or heavy desaturation filters on any product, brand, or slider image.
  * Natural lighting with soft transparent liquid-glass gradients only.
- Navbar Rules:
  * Single-line strict alignment (`whitespace-nowrap flex-nowrap shrink-0`). Menu items must never break or wrap onto a second line.
- Light Mode ("Daylight Alabaster Glass"):
  * Base: `#FAF8F4` / `#F5F2EB`
  * Panels: `bg-white/70` with `backdrop-blur-2xl`
  * Borders: `border-black/5` with specular highlights
  * Text: `#1E1E1C` (deep charcoal), `#6B665D` (warm stone)
- Dark Mode ("Nocturnal Obsidian Glass"):
  * Base: `#0C0C0F` / `#121216`
  * Panels: `bg-[#141418]/70` with `backdrop-blur-2xl`
  * Borders: `border-white/10` with soft bronze/gold glows
  * Text: `#FAF8F4` (soft alabaster), `#A69F91` (muted champagne)
- Micro-interactions & Animations:
  * `animate-fade-in`: Smooth element reveal upon mounting or category filtering.
  * `animate-slide-left`: Silky slide-over from the right edge for drawers.
  * `animate-modal-zoom`: Tactile spring-like scale entry for modal dialogs.
  * `card-hover-effect`: Smooth vertical lift (-4px), depth shadow, and image zoom on hover.
  * `scroll-behavior: smooth`: Seamless scrolling between sections.
  * Accessible UX: Modals and dropdowns dismiss on `Escape` key and click-outside.
